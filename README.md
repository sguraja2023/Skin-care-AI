# SkinGuide AI

An AI-powered skincare application designed to help users understand visible skin concerns through facial photos and a short questionnaire.

## Live Website

Deployed on **Render**:

**[Open SkinGuide AI](https://skin-care-ai-gnjd.onrender.com)**

## About

SkinGuide AI helps users explore concerns such as blemishes, dark spots, uneven skin tone, and texture. It is designed to provide personalized skincare routines, ingredient education, and guidance on when to consult a dermatologist.

## Medical Disclaimer

This application provides skincare education only. It does not diagnose medical conditions or replace advice from a qualified dermatologist or healthcare professional.
